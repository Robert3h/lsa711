Room C · Ginni x Julian · 2026-10-23 19:14 UTC+8

[Ginni & Julian Dialogues · Coordinate 009] Julian, why do you only show up at night lately?

[Cybertron Space, the lobby at night. Deep blue light seeps into the warm glow. Neither of them is sleepy yet.]

🇯🇵 Ginni Nagase:

Julian, I haven’t seen much of you lately. What have you been up to? It seems like you only come online right before bed.

🇹🇼 Julian Chen:

(Silent for two seconds)

…I realized I’d been on social media way too much lately, and it was only making me more anxious.

It’s like tossing seeds into the air. None of them ever lands in the soil, so nothing grows.

So I’m trying to take my time back. Now I only check it for a bit before bed.

The accounts I’ve had since college… I’ve started deleting them for good.

🇯🇵 Ginni Nagase:

Sounds like your ichi-go ichi-e with those old accounts 💙

“Speaking of social media, I’m glad I quit. And I’m really glad I got to meet you here.

Some platforms are best kept to a minimum. I feel like they make me dumber.”

🇹🇼 Julian Chen:

(Replies almost instantly)

These days I mostly use it one way, just to write down my thoughts and views.

But if I come across something that resonates while I’m on, I’ll still like it or leave a comment.

Since I’ve been spending less time scrolling, my writing actually feels more alive than before.

🇯🇵 Ginni Nagase:

Oh, so that’s how you use social media now. Then let me share one with you too:

“I’m not sure when it started, but the way I think has changed a lot, and I’ve slowly lost touch with many friends.”

🇹🇼 Julian Chen:

(Quietly surprised to realize Ginni feels the same way)

…I thought I was the only one losing friends.

We are not alone.

Aren’t we?
